## 1. Sales Analysis

Tools: 
Power BI, DAX, Google Sheets

Sales Dashboard
An interactive dashboard analysing 8,399 sales transactions (2014 to 2017). I combined four yearly CSV files into a single fact table and built a star schema with six dimension tables (date, product, customer, ship mode, order priority and salesperson).

Business questions answered
- How much revenue does the business generate, and is it growing?
- Are there seasonal patterns?
- How balanced is the sales team?
- Which shipping methods and product containers drive revenue?

Headline results: $14.7M total revenue, 5,516 orders, about $2,672 average order value, and a 5% average discount.

## 2. Budget Analysis

Tools 
Power BI, DAX, Excel

National Budget Visual

A four-page Power BI report analysing the Nigerian federal budget by Ministries, Departments and Agencies (MDAs). It covers a national overview plus deep dives into the Presidency, the Ministry of Defence and the Ministry of Finance, splitting allocation into Capital, Overhead and Personnel.

Business questions answered
- How is the national budget split between capital, overhead and personnel spending?
- Which MDAs receive the largest allocations?
- How much of the budget goes to core versus non-core functions?
- How does the spending mix differ between individual ministries?

Headline results: about 46T total allocation, split roughly 13T capital, 19T overhead and 14T personnel.

Key insights
- Core functions take about 91% of the total budget.
- The Ministry of Finance holds the largest single allocation (about 18T, roughly 40% of the total), mostly in overhead.
- Personnel costs dominate smaller bodies, at about 77% of the Presidency's allocation and about 71% of the Ministry of Defence's.
