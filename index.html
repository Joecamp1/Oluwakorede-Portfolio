Nigeria National Budget Analysis

  • Power BI
  • DAX
  • Excel

A four-page Power BI report analysing the national budget by ministry, department and agency, with deep dives into the Presidency, Defence and Finance.

  • Core functions take about 91% of the total budget.
  • The Ministry of Finance holds the largest allocation, about 18T or roughly 40% of the total.
  • Personnel costs make up about 77% of the Presidency’s allocation and 71% of Defence’s.
